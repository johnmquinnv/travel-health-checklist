# Travel Health Checklist

**Maintainer:** John Quinn, MD, MPH, PhD, EMT-P  
**Version:** 1.2.0 · **Last editorial update:** 31 October 2026  
**Clinical review status:** educational draft; independent clinical sign-off is not recorded. Local review and approval are required before operational use.

Practical, copy-ready checklists for the three phases of a trip: before, during and after.

> Educational and general. Not medical advice. Always check current destination-specific guidance and see a clinician or travel clinic for personal advice.

## Authoritative sources

Checklists were reviewed against the following (check them for current, destination-specific advice):

- [CDC Travelers' Health](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel)
- [CDC Yellow Book](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/)
- [WHO Travel and health](https://www.who.int/health-topics/travel-and-health)
- [ECDC](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en)
- [NHS: Sunscreen and sun safety](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/seasonal-health/sunscreen-and-sun-safety/) — source for SPF/UVA protection and reapplication guidance.

Items under "General good practice" are common-sense suggestions that are not taken directly from these sources.

## How to use

Copy the checklist into your notes app or print it. Tick items off, and adapt them to your destination, itinerary and health needs.
