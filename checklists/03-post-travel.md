# Post-travel checklist

- [ ] Finish any course of malaria prophylaxis as directed
- [ ] Monitor for fever, diarrhoea, rash, cough or other new symptoms for several weeks
- [ ] Seek medical review for any illness after travel and tell the clinician where you travelled, when, and what you did
- [ ] Seek review for symptoms that develop weeks or months later
- [ ] Update your vaccination record
- [ ] Replace used first-aid supplies and review what worked or did not
- [ ] Keep notes for next time: insurer experience, local services, items you wished you had
